Questions, answered

Good to know before you book.

How much does junk removal or a dumpster rental cost?

We quote up front and honestly, with no junk fees. Pricing depends on the size of the load or container and where you are in our service area. Tell us about your project and we'll text or call you back with an exact number.

Do you offer same-day service?

Often, yes. Same-day and next-day pickup or delivery is available when our schedule allows. Call (706) 676-6170 early in the day for the best shot at a same-day slot.

What's the difference between junk removal and a dumpster rental?

With junk removal, our crew does all the lifting and loading and hauls everything away the same day. With a dumpster rental, we drop a roll-off container at your place, you fill it on your own schedule, and we haul it off when you're done.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Rome and serve Floyd, Bartow, Polk, Gordon, Chattooga and Haralson counties in Georgia, plus Cherokee County, Alabama.

Is 180 licensed and insured?

Yes. 180 is locally owned, licensed and insured, operating under USDOT 4023227, so your property and project are protected.

What can't go in a dumpster?

Hazardous materials, wet paint, tires, batteries, and certain appliances with refrigerant need special handling. Not sure about an item? Just ask and we'll point you the right way.
