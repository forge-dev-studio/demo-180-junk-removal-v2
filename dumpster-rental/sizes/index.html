How to choose the right size

Picking the right roll-off comes down to two things: how much you are throwing away, and how heavy it is. Lighter, bulkier debris like furniture, household clutter and light remodel waste fills space fast, so you will want more volume. Heavy material like concrete, dirt, shingles and tile is better suited to a smaller container with a higher weight allowance.

The 10-yard is our compact workhorse, perfect for a single room, a small bathroom remodel, or heavy debris jobs. The 15-yard is the most popular all-rounder for garage and basement cleanouts. Step up to the 20-yard for larger remodels and roof tear-offs, and the 30-yard for new construction and whole-home projects.

What size dumpster do I need?

Pick by how much you are throwing away and how heavy it is. Lighter, bulkier debris fills space fast, so go bigger on volume. Heavy material like concrete or shingles suits a smaller container with a higher weight allowance. Not sure? Call and we will recommend the right size.

How long can I keep the dumpster?

We work around your project. Tell us your timeline when you book and we will set a window that fits, with flexible pickup when you are done.

Can the dumpster sit on my driveway?

Yes. Our drivers place boards to protect the surface. Just point to where you want it and make sure there is clear overhead and side access for the truck.
