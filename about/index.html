180 Junk Removal & Dumpster Rentals is a locally owned company based in Rome, Georgia, serving homeowners, contractors and businesses across Floyd County and the wider Northwest Georgia region.

The name says it: we are about the turnaround. We take a cluttered garage, a gutted kitchen, a packed-out rental or a debris-covered job site and flip it a full 180, fast, clean and without the runaround.

Why neighbors choose 180

Locally owned & operated

We live and work here. When you call, you reach a local crew that knows these towns, not a national call center.

Honest, up-front pricing

We do the lifting

With full-service junk removal, our crew walks the property, gives you a price, and loads everything onto the truck. You do not lift a finger.
